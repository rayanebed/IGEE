GIGAKICK Martial Arts Academy: A Modern Approach to Striking and MMA Training

As mixed martial arts continues to grow worldwide, training philosophies inside gyms are evolving as well. Today, many academies focus not only on physical conditioning, but also on technical precision, discipline, and long-term skill development. One example of this modern approach is GIGAKICK Martial Arts Academy, a martial arts gym founded by professional UFC fighter Giga Chikadze.

Who Is Giga Chikadze?

Giga Chikadze is a professional mixed martial artist competing in the UFC’s featherweight division. Known for his high-level striking, Chikadze has a background in traditional martial arts and kickboxing, which has heavily influenced his fighting style inside the cage. Beyond competition, Chikadze has spent years training, coaching, and refining striking systems that emphasize timing, distance management, and technical efficiency rather than raw aggression.

What Is GIGAKICK Martial Arts Academy?

GIGAKICK Martial Arts Academy is a mixed martial arts gym built around the striking philosophy developed through Giga Chikadze’s professional career. The academy offers training programs in disciplines such as:

  • Kickboxing
  • Karate
  • Self-defense
  • Mixed Martial Arts (MMA)

The focus of training is structured learning, technical development, and discipline, making it suitable for a wide range of practitioners — from beginners and youth students to experienced martial artists and competitive fighters.

Training Philosophy and Approach

Unlike fitness-only gyms, GIGAKICK Martial Arts Academy emphasizes:

  1. Technical striking fundamentals
  2. Distance control and movement
  3. Fight IQ and situational awareness
  4. Progressive skill development over time

This approach reflects the training methods used at the professional level, adapted for students of different ages and experience levels. The goal is to build strong foundations before advancing to higher-intensity training.

Who Is GIGAKICK Designed For?

The academy’s training structure supports:

  • Kids learning discipline and coordination
  • Teens developing confidence and technique
  • Adults training for fitness or martial arts mastery
  • Advanced athletes interested in professional-level striking concepts

By focusing on fundamentals first, the academy promotes safe, sustainable progression rather than short-term results.

Location and Online Presence

GIGAKICK Martial Arts Academy operates in Costa Mesa,California and maintains an official online presence where more information about its philosophy and programs can be found.

For more details, visit their official website: GIGAKICK Martial Arts Academy

Why Founder-Led Gyms Matter in MMA

Gyms founded by active or former professional fighters often provide unique insight into real competition demands. In the case of GIGAKICK Martial Arts Academy, the connection to Giga Chikadze allows students to learn techniques and principles shaped by years of elite-level experience.

This founder-driven model is becoming increasingly relevant as martial artists seek training environments that balance tradition, modern MMA knowledge, and structured education.
